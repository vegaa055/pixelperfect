About the studio

We are, in a word,
observers.

Pixel Perfect is an independent art studio working across two languages: the honest grain of black-and-white photography and the deliberate brushwork of digital portraiture. We chase the quiet, in-between moments most people walk past.

Every commission begins with a conversation and ends with a piece made to be lived with — printed, framed, and looked at for years. No filters standing in for feeling.
